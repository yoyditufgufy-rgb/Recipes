Grilled Shrimp & Avocado Quinoa Salad 🥗🍤

Fresh, protein-packed and ready in minutes, with a zesty pesto vinaigrette.

🛒 Ingredients

  • Large shrimp · 1 lb, peeled and deveined
  • Cooked quinoa · 1.5 cups, fluffed and cooled
  • Arugula / baby greens · 2 cups
  • Cherry tomatoes · 1 cup, halved
  • Ripe avocado · 1 large, diced
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper · for searing
  • Basil pesto · 3 tbsp
  • Extra virgin olive oil · 3 tbsp
  • Lemon juice · 2 tbsp, fresh
  • Garlic · 1 clove, minced

👩‍🍳 Step by step

  1. 1

    Whisk the dressing. Mix pesto, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt and pepper in a jar. Shake well and set aside.

  2. 2

    Prepare the base. Layer the cooled quinoa and arugula in a large bowl and toss gently.

  3. 3

    Sear the shrimp. Pat shrimp dry, season with salt, pepper and oil. Cook on a hot grill pan 2–3 minutes per side until pink and opaque.

  4. 4

    Assemble. Top the base with shrimp, halved tomatoes and diced avocado.

  5. 5

    Drizzle and serve. Spoon the pesto vinaigrette over everything and serve right away, with extra dressing on the side.
